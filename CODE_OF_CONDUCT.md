# Community Code of Conduct

KathaSvara welcomes respectful contributions from people of all backgrounds and skill levels.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, inclusive and patient. Critique technical choices rather than personal identities. Respect contributors' time, creator rights, languages, accessibility needs and voice-consent boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publication of personal information, unwanted sexual attention, malicious impersonation and unauthorized sharing of voice recordings are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately through the [contact page](https://sanskarin.github.io/contact/#send-a-message). Maintainers may request changes, hide harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Reports will be evaluated as discreetly and fairly as practical.
