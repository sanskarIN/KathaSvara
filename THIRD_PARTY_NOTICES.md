### Distribution rule

No model weights, training data or other third-party media are committed or bundled. Verify current model-card license, commercial/redistribution terms and third-party dependency inventory before creating an installer. Any future sound/music assets must have a recorded source, license and attribution.

Chatterbox-generated audio may include an upstream perceptual watermark. Do not claim to remove or bypass that watermark.
